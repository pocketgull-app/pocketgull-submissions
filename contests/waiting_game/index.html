02. 3D Continuous Slice Depth & Spatial Encoding

Extracting Continuous Depth & Canonical Medial-Lateral Symmetry from DICOM Headers
SPECIMEN BENCH 02
To endow a 2.5D vision backbone with continuous 3D anatomical coordinates without the VRAM explosion of 3D convolutions, we compute the normalized slice projection directly from standard DICOM geometry in 4 deterministic steps:
01. ACQUISITION NORMAL VECTOR (0020,0037) IOP
Cross product of row and column directional cosines r, c:
n = r × c = (r_y c_z - r_z c_y, r_z c_x - r_x c_z, r_x c_y - r_y c_x)
02. SLICE SPATIAL PROJECTION (0020,0032) IPP
Project 3D origin p_i = (x_i, y_i, z_i) onto unit normal n:
z_i = p_i · n = x_i n_x + y_i n_y + z_i n_z
03. MIN-MAX SPAN CALIBRATION [0.0, 1.0] Bounds
Normalize across all slices j ∈ [1, N] in the series:
z_norm(i) = (z_i - min(z)) / (max(z) - min(z))
04. LATERALITY CANONICALIZATION 0.0=Medial, 1.0=Lateral
Align left and right knees onto a single canonical anatomical axis:
z_canon(i) = z_norm(i) [Right] | 1.0 - z_norm(i) [Left]
